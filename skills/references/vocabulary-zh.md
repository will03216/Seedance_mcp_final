# Vocabulary Zh

Use this file when the user wants prompt-ready Chinese film language.

## Shot sizes

- 大远景: extreme wide shot
- 远景: wide shot
- 全景: full shot
- 中景: medium shot
- 中近景: medium close-up
- 近景: close-up
- 特写: extreme close-up
- 过肩镜头: over-the-shoulder

## Camera movement

- 固定机位: locked-off
- 推镜头: dolly push-in
- 拉镜头: dolly pull-out
- 摇镜头: pan
- 升降摇: tilt
- 环绕: orbit
- 跟拍: tracking follow
- 手持镜头: handheld
- 稳定器跟拍: steadicam
- 一镜到底: one-take
- 甩镜: whip pan
- 焦点转换: rack focus

## Lighting and atmosphere

- 主光: key light
- 辅光 / 补光: fill light
- 轮廓光: rim light
- 逆光: backlight
- 柔光: soft light
- 硬光: hard light
- 黄金时刻: golden hour
- 冷蓝调: cool blue
- 暖琥珀色: warm amber
- 薄雾: thin fog
- 浮尘微粒: dust motes
- 呼气白雾: breath mist

## Style and texture

- 变形宽银幕镜头: anamorphic lens
- 球面镜头: spherical lens
- 胶片颗粒: film grain
- 低饱和色调: muted palette
- 去饱和: desaturated
- 高对比: high contrast
- 赛璐璐渲染: cel-shaded
- 水彩渲染: watercolor wash
- 照片级写实: photorealistic

## Motion and timing

- 慢动作 / 升格: slow motion
- 正常速度: real-time
- 延时摄影: time-lapse
- 卡点: beat-sync
- 缓入: ease in
- 缓出: ease out
- 匹配剪辑: match cut

## Filter-safer action wording

- 冲击: impact
- 碰撞: collision
- 格挡: parry
- 反击: counter-move
- 闪避: evade
- 能量爆发: energy release
- 冲击波扩散: shockwave ripple
- 火花迸溅: sparks cascade

## Ready-made phrases

- `中景缓推，情绪逐渐收紧`
- `低角度跟拍，地面灰尘被动作带起`
- `单侧主光，另一侧保持深阴影`
- `环绕主体，距离保持稳定`
- `薄雾与冷蓝环境光形成层次`
